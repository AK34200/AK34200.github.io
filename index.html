About Me ?

4th-year Video Game student at ESMA Montpellier, specializing in game design, with hands-on experience in level design and sound design. A lifelong gamer, I’ve been designing game concepts since middle school: systems, rules, and coherent worlds. To test my ideas, I prototype in Unreal Engine 5, which has also taught me the technical constraints of development. Today, in team projects, I contribute to mechanics design, level design, ideation, prototyping, and iteration, and I occasionally work on sound design, always with one goal: improving the player experience. Curious and creative, I love analyzing games, experimenting, and turning concepts into tangible play experiences. A big Rayman fan, inspired by its game design and art direction… still waiting for a Rayman 4.
